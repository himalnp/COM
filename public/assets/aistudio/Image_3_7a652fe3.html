Why Surkhet Computer Institute?

For over a decade, our institute has stood as Karnali Province’s premier vocational computer training establishment. Situated right in Birendranagar’s academic quarter, we bridge the divide between theoretical university syllabi and the concrete technical competencies mandated by federal, provincial, and private employers across Nepal.

  • check_circle Mid-Western University Proximity & Alignment: Direct coaching modules mapped directly to MU and TU examinations.
  • check_circle Provincial Loksewa Specialization: Custom mock labs calibrated to the Karnali Public Service Commission standards.
  • check_circle Authorized Certification: Nationally accepted credentials for government registration and vocational accreditation.
